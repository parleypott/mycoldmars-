THE DECK — full instrument. The script sits inside a recessed readout window; the timecode is the hero, ivory digits in a warm display. The most tactile reading of the brief. THE FRAME — the chassis is only a thin hardware rail around a calm broadsheet reading column. Timecode sits quietly in the margin as mono type. Reading-first. CARTRIDGES — every block is its own movable hardware module with a numbered tab and a drag spine. Timecode is a small LCD inset. Modular, Notion-like. ONE ACCENT — strict monochrome discipline. A single colour, reserved only for the live timecode and the active control. Calm prose, one hot point. SEGMENT — the real-deck feeling pushed all the way: true 7-segment readouts for every number. Loud instrument chrome, but prose kept calm and legible.
