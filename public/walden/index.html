How to use this ›
  • Pick a draw tool (top-left of the map). Pool, box, freeform shape, or a measuring line.
  • Rectangles & pools drop into the center of the view — then drag to move, grab a corner to resize, or the top dot to rotate.
  • Shapes & measure lines: click to drop points, double-click to finish. Drag any vertex after.
  • Every object shows live dimensions & area in feet. Edit the numbers in the object card for exact sizes.
  • Turn the buildable envelope on to see where things can legally sit. Objects glow red when they cross a setback or the lot line.
  • Flip GIS layers on/off — aerials by year, contours, building footprints, wetlands, flood, soils, slopes and more.
