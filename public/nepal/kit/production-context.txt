# Production context — condensed from the T.H.E. Nepal production book + overview

## Johnny's theme statement (overview.rtf)
"The blessing in the curse of a new road." Roads are both lifeline and threat: markets,
medicine, schools, tourists, Jumla apples, pilgrim flows — against landslides, deadly
single-lane cliff roads, environmental damage, and social disruption. Geopolitical layer:
India and China competing to extend corridors; Kathmandu pursuing strategic autonomy;
speed of construction trades against engineering and resilience. Observable shifts to
profile: farmers→entrepreneurs (apples), risky logistics tied to road status, GENDER AND
HOUSEHOLD CHANGES (men migrate, women run farms and markets), governance grievances.

## Scope of story (Johnny's own words, condensed)
1. A story about WOMEN in the Karnali valley: kids and/or husbands leave for work, women
   are left to keep surviving.
2. Juxtaposition: documenting/celebrating old culture and traditions VS the road and
   modernity rapidly changing that culture. Must-have: a traditional practice on camera
   (dress, music, dance, food, ritual, the traditional pipe lesson).
3. Two days deep in one village: Simikot chosen (Okhren the alternate; nearly every
   family there has kids who left).
4. Characters: JABA SHAHI is the main character (best camera presence, good sound bites,
   owns a store). Also "the woman at 1:03" of the scout edit: had many children,
   everyone left; strong presence; revisit her.
5. Monastery: the story is about women along the highway, so the WOMEN-ONLY monastery
   (Yalbang nunnery) matters; possibly stay at the male one. Rituals 6am and 3pm at both.
Goal: "real immersion in the lives of these woman, a documentation of Karnali culture,
and a honest look at what its changing and the positive and negative of those changes."

## Shoot days (Sept 22-29)
- D1 Sep 22: KTM→Surkhet. 4pm trailer hero shot (prayer flags, tripod, for Sky).
- D2 Sep 23: Surkhet→Pilchaur, 9-10h drive. Scenes: construction/road workers, villagers
  traveling, truck drivers (ride a lorry), traditional music/dress. Blacktopping at
  Pilchaur: fresh pavement + machinery against rugged landscape. Nepali spoken.
- D3 Sep 24: Pilchaur→Simikot, 9-10h. District HQ check-in. 7pm: JH lands at Jaba's
  store, meets Jaba, she invites him to X (festival? community event? dinner?).
- D4 Sep 25: Full Simikot day embedding with Jaba + other women. Apple farming, drone
  at first light. Sunrise 6:07.
- D5 Sep 26: Morning apple harvest with farmer near Okhren/Simikot. → Yalbang Monastery,
  3pm ritual. Nunnery is a parallel community; climate-monitoring site; Tibetan spoken.
  Full shoot access; sleeping arrangement inside monastery unconfirmed until arrival;
  guesthouse 5 min walk.
- D6 Sep 27: Yalbang→Hilsa border (RAP day, NO DRONE within 5km of border/airstrips).
  11:30-2:30 filming border. 6h drive back to Simikot "and reflect on the journey."
  Stay with Jaba or guesthouse near store.
- D7 Sep 28: Fixed-wing out morning. 2pm KTM: Karnali apples in a Kathmandu market,
  vendor interview, close the economic loop.
- D8 Sep 29: fly home.
