# JABA CARD
*read before every visit: D3, D4, D6*

## 8 QUESTIONS, IN ORDER
1. What was the heaviest load you ever carried, before the road? Show me how.
2. Tell me about the day the first truck reached Simikot. Where were you standing?
3. Tell me about your sons. What does your youngest do in Surkhet? Do you remember the morning he left? What did you cook?
4. Where is your husband right now, today? What was the last thing you two did together?
5. The road made life easier, and your family is separated. Which do you think about more?
6. You're smiling while you say that. Why? *(THE question. Never skip. Never early.)*
7. If giving back the road would bring your sons home, would you? *(Sit in whatever comes.)*
8. Will they come home for Dashain? *("Not this year" is the wound. Either answer is the episode.)*

**Exits, every visit:** What did I not ask? Anything you don't want in the film?

## 4 HARD RULES
- **Raw line → silence.** Hold her face ten full seconds. Translator stays quiet too.
- **Second question goes INTO the wound, smaller:** "what did you cook that night," "where were you standing." Never "how does that feel."
- **No giggle exit.** She laughs when it hurts and will offer you the way out. Decline it. Ask about the smile.
- **Reshoot the scout two-shot:** her behind the counter, the space beside her empty. Then film her watching the old clip. "What do you see?"

## 3 SCENES WITH HER
1. **Inventory walk.** Five objects, shelf by shelf: cost now, cost before, how she carried it then. Once talking, once silent and tight.
2. **The message.** Play her son's video, phone to phone. Ask permission on camera. Hold her face; ten seconds; second question.
3. **The empty rooms.** She shows the house. Whose bed was whose. Shoot only what she offers, tight, daylight. If she goes quiet, the quiet is the scene.
