"Full shelves, empty house."

The sentence. Every scene proves it, complicates it, or gets cut. The film is about separation, not roads.

The one assignment

Once a day, when something true is happening, notice the urge to speak, and stay. Ten seconds. Then the smaller question: what did you cook that night, where were you standing. Never "how does that feel."

With Jaba · the four rules

  1. Raw line → ten seconds of silence. Translator too.
  2. When she smiles over the wound: "You're smiling while you tell me this. Why?" Then wait.
  3. No giggle exits. She will offer you the way out. Decline it.
  4. Reshoot the scout two-shot, the space beside her empty. Then film her watching the old clip: "What do you see?"

Son's message: fixer calls ahead from KTM. Film him in Surkhet Day 1, carry the video three days up the road, play it in her store. Consent at every step; a refusal is also a scene.

Morning reminders

  1. Ten seconds before you speak.
  2. The obstacle is the scene. Blocked road = get out and meet people.
  3. Interview inside the line before solving your way past it.
  4. Camera away? Phone rolls inside the car.
  5. Ask the question out loud to someone, every day.
  6. Turn to Niroj or Ashish on camera: "What am I looking at?"
  7. Food and music get real questions. Every meal, every song.
  8. Find the one who gave up.
  9. Small crucial things get a tight shot in daylight.
  10. Shoot an ending shaped like a person.
