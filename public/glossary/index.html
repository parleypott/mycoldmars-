Teenage Soul

say "build this in teenage soul" — or hand over the card + tokens ↓

The soul of a Teenage Engineering box and a fig.01 technical drawing, in service of human storytelling. Warm cream paper, thin ink hairlines, registration-screw corners, monospace micro-labels, color-coded by function. Every block is a tactile hardware cartridge. Flat on purpose — no shadows, no gloss. It feels like a beloved instrument built for one person, not an app.

warm · tactile · precise · flat · instrument-like · analog · understated · function-coded · built-for-one
