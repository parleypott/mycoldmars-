Teenage Soul

say "build this in teenage soul" — or hand over the card ↓

It's a sun-faded 45 sleeve found in a teenager's bedroom. Big, unguarded emotion with no irony — the way you feel things at fifteen — carried on the warmth of soul music: analog tape, gospel roots, a needle dropping on vinyl. The exact opposite of clean, cold, corporate. For a Myanmar human story it makes you feel your way in before you're explained a thing.

yearning · warm · sincere · worn · intimate · soulful · unguarded · handmade · nostalgic-but-alive
