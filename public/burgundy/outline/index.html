the whole novel, visualized · the middle-section engine · four ways from the tunnels to the climax
This is the shape of the book. The top is the arc — every chapter, what it does, and its emotional turn (red = new chapter needed, amber = rework). Then the four architectures for the middle, laid out beat by beat, so you can see and pick the path (or the blend). The climax is fixed; the question the whole page answers is how we earn our way to it.

Four ways from the tunnels to the climax

Path 1

The Page That Hears

The all-seeing King rules by a listening network that is deaf to machines, so the only trace Burgundy's growing robot army leaves is a discrepancy in his mother's steel ledger — and the prince who built the robots to be SEEN spends his last year running the audit of his own treason, making himself unhearable, until the Vicentennial forces the books closed and stages a tunnel-survey that would walk inspectors into South-12, and the boy who wanted to be seen wins the crown by the most invisible act imaginable.
engine: WANT-CURDLES-INTO-WEAPON, metered by a ledger. The spine is the canon-locked want: Burgundy assumes he will be crowned but plans to do it DIFFERENTLY — reveal the robots at the Vicentennial itself, undeniable, so his father must finally SEE him and the recited road (rite, tour, married off, shipped away) is rearranged. The middle escalates because the mechanism that should carry the reveal is the same mechanism that forecloses it. Each build escalation consumes more cartel steel, so Mira over-orders, so the household ledger's discrepancy grows in lockstep with the army — and the ledger is the ONLY sense-organ the King has for the machine, because his whole doctrine is control-through-omniscience via a human listening network that is structurally deaf to a robot that 'says nothing back.' The father reads the discrepancy as Mira's cartel graft and sends a finance minister. Burgundy's move (canon-flagged) is to volunteer to run the household reconciliation himself, shielding his mother and capping his trade training — now he writes the reconciliations and steers the hunt, and his competence and his exposure rise on one curve. The turn that kills the want: arming the robots (the militant's demand) blows a hole no festival-provisioning story can paper, which means the machine can now only be read as treason on a page before it is ever received as a gift at the rite. The boy who built a machine to be SEEN can survive only by becoming perfectly unseen — mastering the lie is how he loses the self he wanted seen. The clock is the Vicentennial: the rite that crowns him also forces the books closed AND stages a ceremonial tunnel-survey that would map South-12. It tightens every chapter because he is working harder and harder at the thing that will hang him, and can feel the reveal he built it all for dying in his hands.
after: Primary: The Traitor Baru Cormorant — competence and exposure on one curve; Burgundy volunteers to audit his own treason exactly as Baru masters a province's books to betray it. Braided with The Count of Monte Cristo for architecture — interleaved fuses cross-cut so one always spikes while another lays groundwork, dramatic irony as the load-bearer (the father grousing to the son who ordered the steel), the scheduled public ceremony everyone counts toward as the rigged detonation date. Catching Fire supplies the displaced near-miss (the catch lands on Mira, not Burgundy) and the compliance-accelerates-catastrophe spine. Distinct from the original path in that the case-study machinery is subordinated to the LOCKED want-engine — the ledger vise is how the peaceful-reveal dream dies, not the point itself.
1. THE GIFT REJECTED / THE WANT INSTALLED (17th birthday) Age 17 · Vicentennial ~3 yrs off (pre-runway; the seed year)
build No factory yet. The single mining DREKKO stands finished in the basement on the night after his 17th Twinrise. Ch1-2 canon: he means to carry it to his first council and set it before his father, not as rebellion but as a GIFT, the mark he intends to make, modernization that raises output and spares boys the chisel. The build that matters this beat is the notebooks: a hundred machines, a number with his name on it.
train Training begins here (canon-locked at 17). Ch2 is the first lesson: the King rejects the robot not for output but because it 'digs quietly and tells me nothing.' Burgundy learns the doctrine before he learns the world, control through omniscience, labor as a listening network. Mira then shows the room and reframes training as COVER: be flawless in daylight so your father never looks at your nights.
father The doctrine IS the closing-in, planted at its coldest and quietest: 'I know what is moving under this planet before it moves,' said in the very scene where he has just learned his son built a machine for months undetected. The reader alone holds the irony. The father's omniscience has exactly one deaf spot, and it is the thing on the table.
bond The want, load-bearing and locked: he built this to be SEEN, to show his father a forward-looking prince and maybe rearrange the recited road (rite at 20, then the tour, married off, shipped away, a fate he has low-key dreaded his whole life). The rejection wounds because the gift was the self, offered, and read as a blind spot. We are the only ones who see the boy under the machine.
tension TIER-1, recoverable: he pockets the plan instead of the robot. The want survives the rejection, quieter, aimed now at the Vicentennial itself, three years out. The clock is set in the father's mouth.
2. THE WORLD LEARNED / THE FIRST STEEL (17→18) Age 17→18 · Vicentennial ~2.5 yrs off (the training-rhythm middle)
build The idea forms across the double-life stretch: the mining DREKKOs already work the seam, so idle hands could build MORE robots. Steel, rail, bearings begin flowing through Mira's channels, still modest, still coverable as estate provisioning. This is the thin middle the training rhythm exists to carry.
train ARCHIVES (the bearded archivist) then EXTERNAL AFFAIRS (Schwartz), three weeks each, canon rotations landing in their proper home. Through them he learns the world the way canon demands, not tutor-flashbacks: the Great Rupture, the broken old clans now sitting in the resistance, the tribute economy, the grey valve the core keeps open on purpose. The archivist's small WORRY when the boy asks who checks the checkers.
father The seed as an offhand joke: at council the King grouses that provisioning to the estates has run heavy, that Mira's cartel 'never met a bearing it didn't triple-bill.' He means it as a jab at her people. Burgundy, who ordered the bearings, laughs with him. Both halves held by the reader alone.
bond Mira as the un-seeing parent, canon-exact: he tries to show her the first finished rank, wanting her to SEE what he has made. She answers with a fresh manifest and a colder shipping route. Her love is the over-order; the distance is the shape her love was taught to take. The rhyme under it, planted: the Vicentennial that sold her into this cage is the same rite now coming for her son.
tension TIER-1: a provisions clerk queries a duplicate steel order; Mira makes it vanish before it is a question. The membrane exists and can tear. Training rhythm keeps the reader moving; the steel keeps the dread accruing.
3. THE TWO KEYS DEPOSITED (18→19) Age 18→19 · Vicentennial ~1.5 yrs off (the last training year)
build South-12 turns from mine toward factory in principle; throughput climbs; the double life hardens into routine. The want sharpens into a plan with a shape: reveal the robots at the Vicentennial, undeniable, so the father cannot dismiss the gift a second time.
train The two rotations that arm the endgame, deposited now so they pay off live later. DEFENSE-MINISTER: the weapons depot exists (canon: learned in a King-lesson), its back unguarded, Nicholas not worried anyone here could reach it. TRADE-MINISTER: the ledger craft itself, how provisions are logged, how festival inflation hides overspend, how a reconciliation is built and signed. He walks out holding the depot and the pen that can hide it.
father The gripe hardens into intent: before next year's reckoning the household books are 'off enough to be embarrassing,' and the King will send a finance minister to walk Mira's provisions line by line. Said as housekeeping. Burgundy feels the floor tilt, because he now knows exactly how a reconciliation reads, and his is a lie waiting to be signed.
bond The father as ammunition, canon warmth planted so the patricide costs: teaching the depot and the craft, the King is briefly, genuinely proud, calls him the finish on five generations. Cruelty and tenderness in one coat. The reader must half-love him here.
tension TIER-2 opens: the audit is now a scheduled event with a date, not a joke at council. The last training year ends with both keys in his hand and the clock at eighteen months.
4. THE 19th BIRTHDAY — THE DOOR AND THE LONELINESS (the pivot) Age 19 · Vicentennial 1 yr off (preserve; runway begins)
build STOP the machinery. The factory hums offstage. Canon preserve-opening: he wakes alone on his birthday, reads an off-world manual from one of Mira's crates, passes the kitchens and hears Tuck's party without him.
train Rotations are effectively done; he is nearly the perfect prince. The father's toast is all Vicentennial, the crown coming in a year, the clock in the King's own mouth. Then the offhand cruelty: tomorrow, your first hunt, never asked. The King disposes of his son's time.
father Not the audit tonight but the CAGE: the toast makes the recited road real and near. Crowned, married off, shipped away from the work, one year out. The want (reveal the robots to escape this) and the clock (the rite that seals it) are now the same date.
bond The book's loneliest early beat, canon-exact: the quilt exchange BREAKS for the first time (he never cuts his square, never collects Tuck's). The one connection they had fails the year the clock starts. Do not fix it here. We bond in the fracture. He is lonely in a full room built entirely for him.
tension Existential, not kinetic. The dread turns over from 'will the steel be noticed' to 'the door closes on my 20th birthday and I have one year to make myself impossible to ship away.'
5. THE FACTORY, THE AUDITOR, THE PIVOT (M0–M2) M0–M2 of the runway · ~9 months to the rite
build Tunnels break into South-12 (canon +3 months). Hollow dinners begin: Tiffins via Tuck, off-world food, the broken old clans, the first inch of trust. South-12 becomes a true factory; DREKKOs by the score; materiel demand doubles through Mira's valve.
train Payoff, not new rotation. The finance minister (steerable, insulated by the father's trust, not incorruptible) installs himself at the household books. Burgundy, using the trade-craft he was taught, makes the canon-flagged move: he asks the King to let HIM run the household reconciliation before the rite, framed cleanly as a dutiful prince shielding his mother AND as the natural capstone of his trade training. Both motivations visible, so it reads as duty, not suicide. The King grants it as an honor and a test.
father The vise, canon-grounded and split clean: the King's man hunts MIRA'S STEEL (household provisions), nothing about Nicholas's depot (that stays Nicholas's, offstage, per canon). Burgundy now writes the reconciliations and steers the hunt, feeding the decoy of ordinary cartel over-provisioning. The father, delighted, trusts him more and watches him less, which is exactly what keeps the materiel flowing. The omniscient man's one deaf spot now has his own son standing in it.
bond The reader as sole confidant, milked: every audit scene runs two texts, the loyal son protecting his mother on the surface, the treason-in-progress underneath that only we hold. But the want keeps it warm, not cold: he is still, secretly, building toward the reveal. He tells himself the reconciliation buys him to the Vicentennial, where he will finally be seen.
tension Inverts and deepens: safer AND more exposed. Controlling the hunt means the books must actually balance by the rite, and they cannot, because the army is real. Dramatize as bearings and steel, never spreadsheets, per canon's own directive.
6. THE DEPOT + THE DEATH OF THE PEACEFUL REVEAL (M2–M4, the midpoint wound) M2–M4 · deep freeze, the year's dark
build The militant ('we should put weapons on this') and the Ghai woman ('where is it stored,' she KNOWS the back) crystallize the depot plan. Drilling into the unguarded back begins; the first crates of Nicholas's weapons come out; arming starts. South-12 tips from factory to war-factory. Point of no return.

⏲ the time-bomb: THE VICENTENNIAL CROWN-ACCOUNTING — but named on page one of the runway, not sprung at the end. The rite that crowns the prince first requires the crown to formally re-consecrate its holdings, which is canon-sanctioned as a full ceremonial DESCENT AND MAPPING of the deep workings (South-12 among them), presented at the rite; alongside it, the year's ledger reconciliation (the father's finance audit escalated into ceremonial law) and Nicholas's once-per-twenty-years depot re-inventory (the arsenal recounted at the exact moment the tribute rate resets). All three fall on the eve of the 20th birthday, and any one of them physically walks a surveyor or a Nicholas auditor into the robot factory or the emptied depot. The improvement over the original: this is planted at ~month 3 (the toast and the Archives rotation establish that a Vicentennial always re-maps the workings), so the reader spends the ENTIRE runway watching a blade they already understand descend — the honor-and-cage social clock (crowned, married on the tour, shipped away from the work) and the discovery clock are the same object from early on. What tightens across the back half is the DATE moving forward (the finance minister's thread forcing the accounting earlier than custom) and the auditor's physical distance from the cargo field closing. By the eve, "it has to be that night" is every clock at once — coronation, marriage-exile, ledger, survey, and depot-count all striking together — and the only move left is to break the war before the surveyors go down.
why it works: It keeps the original's correct and highest-value insight — that "surprised and blown away" dies the instant the escalation IS the emotional arc, and that the cheapest, highest-yield bond available is making the reader the sole confidant who holds the gap between the flawless prince everyone reads and the yearning maker only we see. That thesis is right and I preserved it whole. What I hardened are the three places an adversarial read breaks the soft version. (1) Mounting tension: recognition is a FEELING and feelings do not inherently ratchet, so I forced each seeing-scene to also upgrade the catch-mechanism and to cost something irreversible — now the curve climbs instead of looping. (2) Nine-month plausibility: I stopped asserting a "blind spot" and grounded it in the canon distinction the original left on the table — the state watches talk, not tunnels; the imports run on TWO channels (audited palace provisions where Mira is exposed on the small stuff, and off-books cartel cargo-field tonnage that never touches the ledger the minister reads), so the audit can bite for months without finding the army, and the depot empties DOWN into tunnels, never past a surface guard, uninventoried for twenty years by a complacent Nicholas — exactly as canon permits. (3) The bond: I replaced asserted intoxication ("the most intoxicating recognition of his life") with a concrete slip per beat — the sentence Tuck swallows, the "No" that cracks the mask, the grease wiped before anyone thanks him, the square cut from the coat's hem — because the reader bonds at the moment of near-slip, not when the narrator names the feeling. And I let Tuck's DRIFT widen (canon: the quilt exchange breaks at the 19th birthday) into a felt low before the Ch17 repair earns the reunion, instead of skipping to it. The patricide finally costs — but via the son's unmet longing, not the father's hidden warmth: the father's tenderness is always pedagogy (canon: presumptuous, cruelty-as-teaching), so the blade at the end is "he never once saw me and I still wanted him to," which is sharper and cannot read as exoneration. And the fixed refusal-of-tribute ending lands as the culmination of the whole want: seen at last, on his own terms, owned by no machine.
risks: Surviving adversarial findings, ranked. (1) PARKED GEOPOLITICS — the original confidently loads Bill's Delian-pact / tribute-as-leash / black-market-valve model into the rotations, but Johnny explicitly parked all geopolitics content ("don't use Bill's take; I'll give you the real story later"). The rotations must deposit their FUNCTION — one load-bearing key each (Archives = the history that lets him read the Hollow's grievances; defense/King-lesson = the depot + the red phone; trade = the ledger logic + festival timing; Schwartz = the smuggling geography) — while keeping the specific geopolitical CONTENT as placeholder slots for Johnny to fill. Do not bake the parked model in. (2) RECOGNITION STILL HABITUATES if the seeing-beats repeat one shape; the iron rule must hold — every seeing-scene also upgrades the catch-mechanism AND anchors to a distinct physical slip, or the middle loops. (3) MIRA DOUBLE-DUTY — she carries both the deepest recognition ("you are my answer") and the instrument-horror, which can tip into thesis or make her read cold; canon says her love is real and the distance is the SHAPE love was taught to take, so she must comfort as comfort (the head-on-the-shoulder beat must land as genuine refuge) BEFORE the objecthood dawns, or the horror has nothing to spoil. (4) THE AUDIT'S PLAUSIBILITY OVER MONTHS depends entirely on the two-channel laundering; if that beat is cut or fudged, a real finance minister finds industrial tonnage in weeks and the book collapses — it is load-bearing, protect it. (5) "BURGUNDY RUNS THE AUDIT HIMSELF" (Johnny's own open question) reads odd if staged as a prince formally investigating the queen; the safe version is bounded — his trade/treasury rotation puts eyes on it, the presumptuous King (proud that his son has "never been more his") hands it to him half to clear Mira, and Burgundy steers and feeds plausible answers rather than holding a formal warrant. (6) FATHER HALF-LOVED can drift toward softening his present-tense cruelty (the lozenge slap, the men in the hole); hold the pedagogy and the viciousness both, and locate the cost in the son's longing, never in the father earning sympathy.

How the great escalation novels do it (the study behind the paths)

Harry Potter (analyzed primarily through Book 1, Philosopher's Stone, with series-level reinforcement)
engine The Potter middle runs on a THREE-BODY ratchet, not a single rising line: (1) what the protagonist perceives/suspects, (2) what the adult institution will credit, (3) the antagonist quietly advancing. Tension climbs by widening the gap between #1 and #2 while #3 ticks — every escalation makes Harry MORE certain and the authorities LESS willing to listen, until acting alone (breaking the rules) is the only door left, which IS the climax. Three concrete sub-mechanisms drive it: 1) NEED-TO-KNOW DRIP as a rung-ladder. The central secret is released in discrete, escalating installments spaced roughly one per 1-2 chapters: Fluffy the three-headed dog → the trapdoor it guards → "the Philosopher's Stone" (the Gringotts break-in clipping retroactively connects) → Nicolas Flamel → Snape-as-suspect → the obstacle chambers. Each rung is a small dopamine hit that keeps the question alive without spending the danger too fast. The reader always knows the NEXT question, never the answer. 2) A LOW-STAKES INVESTIGATION TASK covering the sag. The Flamel hunt (library searches that FAIL, then pay off via the chocolate-frog card) is deliberate busywork-with-rising-meaning: it keeps the protagonists ACTIVE and agentic through the structurally soft middle, delivers incremental partial-wins, and burns page-time without advancing the mortal danger. It is the middle's engine of motion when the plot proper is idling. 3) TWO BRAIDED PLOTLINES, one 'safe' and public, one secret, that periodically TOUCH. Quidditch (public, scheduled, performance stakes) runs parallel to the Stone mystery — and at the jinxed-broom match the two threads collide: the public arena becomes the site of a covert attack. That collision is the signature move — the safe world keeps getting invaded by the secret one, so relief never fully arrives. Escalation is felt because COST climbs monotonically: lose house points → detention → the Forbidden Forest (where the punishment itself delivers the scariest revelation, Voldemort drinking unicorn blood). The institution's own disciplinary machinery keeps shoving Harry deeper toward the danger.
bond Bonding is FRONT-LOADED as deprivation, then paid out as metered recognition. Five stacked moves: 1) THE WOUND FIRST (the cupboard under the stairs). Before any wonder, Rowling establishes Harry as unseen, unloved, un-belonging — a boy who YEARNS to be seen and to matter. Every subsequent recognition (Hagrid: 'Harry — yeh don't know what yeh are?'; strangers knowing his name) lands as wish-fulfillment BECAUSE the ache was installed first. Critical insight: reactive wonder only bonds when it sits ON TOP of a yearning wound. Awe alone is tourism. 2) THE MIDPOINT WOUND-REACTIVATION (the Mirror of Erised). At the exact center of the book, Rowling STOPS the plot machinery to detonate the core want: Harry sees his dead family in the mirror and can't leave it. The middle's emotional peak is not a plot beat — it's the deprivation made literal. This is the model: the sag-midpoint is where you re-open the wound, not where you add plot. 3) LOCKED CLOSE-THIRD POV. We never leave Harry's head; we know only what he knows. His confusion is ours, his discovery ours — automatic identification, and it also runs the mystery (we can't outpace him). 4) AUDIENCE-SURROGATE WONDER. As orphan/newcomer, the world is explained TO Harry, so his awe is licensed as ours — but always tethered to move #1 so it reads as a starving boy being fed, not a tourist gawking. 5) LOYALTY EARNED UNDER STRESS. Friendship is forged in shared danger ('there are some things you can't share without ending up liking each other' — the troll). The found-family bond, and its later FRACTURES (Ron/Harry falling out, Hermione frozen over the Firebolt), are the emotional stakes braided under the plot clock. And a moral-clarity hook: we bond to Harry's anger at unfairness (Dudley's cruelty, Snape's favoritism). Injustice witnessed is a bonding agent.
steal 1) PUT THE WOUND UNDER THE WONDER (fixes the #1 problem directly). Burgundy 'surprised and blown away' fails for the same reason Harry's awe would fail without the cupboard — reactive wonder must sit on a yearning wound. Give every training rotation (Archives, External Affairs, defense, trade, Oz/preserve) two layers: competence-wonder on the surface AND a private ache underneath — each new master reads him as 'the heir,' a function, never HIM. Meter the recognition he actually wants (Mira seeing him; his father seeing the craft) and mostly withhold it — the canon's 'small budget of one-sided recognitions.' The rotations become a serial demonstration of being un-seen while becoming impressive. 2) BUILD BURGUNDY'S MIRROR OF ERISED at the sag-midpoint. The 19th-birthday/preserve scene (lonely in a full room, Tuck's party roaring without him through the kitchen wall, the quilt exchange breaking) IS his Mirror. Do not put plot there — STOP the build/train machinery and detonate the want-to-be-seen. That is the emotional peak of the middle, exactly where Potter puts its midpoint wound. 3) BRAID TWO CLOCKS AND CONVERGE THEM. The training rotations are the public, legible 'school calendar' metronome (spoken Vicentennial countdowns; Steam/Twinrise/the hunt as seasonal feast-waypoints). The build is the secret danger-clock underneath, measured against it. Engineer the convergence Potter uses: the rotation-calendar's end (the rite, the crown, the marriage-tour that ships him away) and the revolution deadline strike the SAME date (the 20th Twinrise). 'It has to be now' should read as both clocks at once — crowned-and-exiled vs depot-emptied. 4) GIVE THE MIDDLE A FLAMEL-HUNT. Burgundy needs a low-stakes recurring investigation that keeps him agentic through the sag and pays out in rungs: assembling how the extraction machine REALLY works, across rotations — the orrery/tribute-audience scene, the manifest losing weight at each transit joint, the pact→disarmament→tribute-as-leash→black-market-valve→the depot's unguarded back. Space one revelation every couple of chapters; each is a partial-win that feeds the depot plan. This is the engine of motion when the plot idles. 5) THE FATHER'S AUDIT IS McGONAGALL/FILCH — escalate its COST monotonically and braid it with build-wins. Beat 1: an offhand gripe (Mira over-spending on cartel provisions). Beat 2: the finance minister sent to audit the ledgers. Beat 3: a near-catch at the books. Beat 4 (the Forbidden-Forest beat): the Father asks Burgundy DIRECTLY and he must deflect what he knows is all for him — the punishment/interrogation that brings him closest to exposure. Alternate these institution-closes-in beats against build-successes so the reader rides tension-relief-tension, never flat. 6) MAKE THE SECRECY TOOLS DOUBLE AS SCARE-GENERATORS. The workshop, Tuck's Tiffin runs, Mira's smuggling network are Burgundy's invisibility cloak — each enables the secret life AND is the thing that nearly betrays it (Tuck's swallowed sentence; the over-ordered steel and bearings; a Tiffin seen in the wrong hand). Concentrate near-misses on the very apparatus of secrecy. 7) USE TUCK DRIFTING AS THE EMOTIONAL NEAR-MISS. Braid the friendship-strain against the plot near-misses the way Potter braids the Ron/Hermione fractures under the danger — the quilt breaking rhymes with a plot near-catch, and loyalty earned/lost is where the reader bonds hardest. Tuck is Burgundy's found-family, and its fraying is the personal clock ticking under the political one.
The Hunger Games trilogy — with the analysis centered on the MIDDLE of Catching Fire (Victory Tour → Quell announcement → training), which is the single closest structural analog to BURGUNDY: a protagonist forced to perform one public identity while a rebellion she's an involuntary symbol of mounts underneath, all under personal surveillance and a named ceremonial clock. Book 1 (the arena / star-crossed-lovers performance) supplies the performance-bond mechanics; Mockingjay supplies the "object in someone else's machine" theme.
engine The core Collins mechanism is COMPLIANCE-AS-ACCELERATION, and it is the exact engine BURGUNDY is missing. In Catching Fire, Snow assigns Katniss a containment task: go on the Victory Tour and convince the districts (and him) that the berry defiance was love, not rebellion — keep the lid on. The engine is that every action she takes to COMPLY instead accelerates the very thing she's containing. The better she performs the star-crossed lovers, the more potent a symbol of defiance she becomes; the Mockingjay is manufactured out of her attempts to be harmless. So the middle doesn't escalate through the protagonist choosing bigger risks — it escalates because the machine she's obeying is converting her obedience into detonation, and she can feel it happening and can't stop. Layered on top are three ratchets, each turned once per "stage": (1) rising surveillance made personal — impersonal Peacekeepers early, then a new hardline Head Peacekeeper (Thread) tightening District 12, then Snow himself in her home, watching her face for cracks; (2) escalating public set-pieces — the tour stops (District 11's salute and killing, District 12's whipping, the Capitol) each of which must go perfectly and each of which fails to contain the fire; (3) a MID-POINT CLOCK-RESET (the Quarter Quell card, read aloud on live TV) that raises the ceiling and converts a social/political threat into a mortal physical one — she performed the whole tour to avoid the arena, and the ceremony sends her straight back into it. The escalation is a vise, not a staircase: the tension comes from the protagonist working harder and harder at the thing that is killing her.
bond Five stacked mechanisms, all portable to Burgundy. (1) THE READER AS SOLE CONFIDANT. First-person present tense keeps us inside Katniss's real-time calculation, and the bond is built on the GAP between the self everyone on screen sees (the performed lover, the compliant victor) and the self only WE are shown (terrified, furious, scheming). Every other character sees the mask; the reader is the only one who sees her. Being seen by the reader is the whole emotional transaction. (2) LOVE AS PROVISION AND COMPETENCE, not words. We bond to Katniss in the first pages because she hunts to feed Prim and volunteers to die for her — care expressed as capable action. Her tenderness is technical (a clean kill, the right snare). (3) TOKEN OBJECTS THAT HOLD RELATIONSHIPS AND DETONATE — the mockingjay pin, Peeta's burned bread, Rue's flowers. Small physical things recur, accrue charge, and pay off catastrophically. (4) INVOLUNTARY OBJECTHOOD RESISTED. Katniss is endlessly styled, positioned, and made into a symbol by Cinna, Haymitch, the rebellion, and Snow — and her ANGER at being handled is itself a bonding engine. We feel her chafe against being an instrument and insist on her own agency; her refusal to be a pure symbol is what makes her a person. (5) A STRAINED PEER BOND that grounds her humanity and dramatizes her isolation — Rue (who dies), Gale (who drifts), Peeta (whose realness she can't verify). Crucially the strain/drift is not a flaw in the friendship; it IS the loneliness, and the loneliness is what we bond to.
steal FOUR lessons a BURGUNDY writer should steal. (1) MAKE THE FATHER'S TRAINING THE FUEL OF THE REVOLUTION — the Catching-Fire compliance-as-acceleration engine, and it's already latent in your manuscript (ch10: he learns osmarine is "why we're so valuable" while secretly building; the King-doctrine "I know what moves under this planet" delivered in the very scene he's failed to detect a machine his son hid for years). Systematize it across all five 3-week rotations: each professional hands Burgundy a literal KEY to the coup — the Archives give him the Great Rupture history that lets him read the resistance's grievances; External Affairs/Schwartz gives him the tribute-valve smuggling geography he'll ship materiel through; the defense minister's lesson is where he learns the weapons-depot exists and that it's under-guarded; the trade minister teaches him the festival-timing craft ("men don't rebel before a festival") which he'll use to time the uprising; the red-phone briefing hands him the ending. The reader knowing each daytime lesson is a nail in the father's coffin — while the father beams that "the training shows" — is both the escalation AND the dramatic-irony bond. Compliance detonates the thing it's meant to preserve. (2) THE READER IS THE ONE WHO SEES HIM — this literally answers the wound ("to be seen, not read as a function"). Build the bond on the gap between the perfect-prince mask everyone in the book reads and the real Burgundy only the reader is shown; his loneliness (the kitchen pass, the ministers' children he never befriended, Tuck drifting) bonds us precisely because WE see it and no one on the page does. Do not fix the Tuck drift — the drift IS the loneliness, the way Gale's drift and Rue's death are Katniss's. And weaponize the quilt exactly like the mockingjay pin: a recurring token that accrues charge and detonates (the ch17 repair — Tuck's held square + Burgundy's cut from the council-coat hem — is your berry moment; plant and pay it off deliberately). (3) MODEL THE LEDGER AUDIT ON THE GALE WHIPPING — a displaced near-miss that lands on MIRA. The catch should fall on the person protecting him: she's the one over-spending, she's the one the finance minister exposes, and Burgundy's cost is watching his mother incriminated for HIS project, unable to intervene without confessing. He pays in guilt, not capture — which keeps the double life alive while deepening the bond (he cannot protect the one person who sees and arms him). Escalate the audit impersonal → personal → intimate: a distant auditor → the father confiding his suspicion of Mira to Burgundy at council → Burgundy having to lie to his father's face → the suspected-informer scare at the depot. (1) The epigraph frame: every chapter opens with a fragment of Princess Irulan's future history that treats Paul as already-legend, already the dead-and-mythologized Emperor. That gap — loved-yet-instrumentalized, seen-as-function-not-person — is the emotional core; and (c) grief as accelerant: we watch him lose father, home, name, and future, then rebuild from zero, and shared loss cements the bond.

The Count of Monte Cristo — Alexandre Dumas
engine The middle of Monte Cristo is not one rising line — it is a BRAIDED MULTI-FUSE CONVERGENCE MACHINE, and that architecture is the whole trick for beating a sagging middle. Once the Count arrives in Paris, Dumas runs THREE independent revenge plots in parallel, each with a different destruction mechanism and its own fuse-length: Morcerf/Fernand dies by REPUTATION (a buried war-crime exposed in the Chamber of Peers), Danglars dies by GREED (false telegraph signals and unlimited credit bleeding him to bankruptcy), Villefort dies by a BURIED CRIME resurfacing (the child he tried to bury alive) plus poison spreading inside his own house. Because there are always 3+ clocks running, whenever one plot is in a slow, patient, laying-groundwork phase, another is spiking to detonation. Dumas cross-cuts so the reader is never asked to sustain tension on a single thread — the rotation IS the engine. Four mechanical devices ratchet it: (1) DRAMATIC IRONY AS THE PRIMARY LOAD-BEARER — the reader knows the Count is Dantès and the targets don't, so every warm scene where an enemy claps the Count on the shoulder, invites him to dinner, trusts him with a daughter, is silently loaded with doom; the friendliness is the tension. (2) ACCUMULATING PLANTED GUNS — Dumas plants devices hundreds of pages before they fire (Haydée's identity, the telegraph, Bertuccio's vendetta, Benedetto the buried baby now grown, and the exquisite cruelty of the Count teaching Madame de Villefort the very poison chemistry she then uses); tension is measured by the number of un-fired guns the reader is holding. (3) SOCIAL-EVENT DETONATION DATES — the fuses don't burn to random moments, they burn to fixed public ceremonies the Count has secretly rigged: a betrothal-contract signing, a Chamber session, a trial. The scheduled festivity is the bomb. (4) PLAN-EXCEEDS-PLANNER — critically, Dumas refuses pure omnipotence: Madame de Villefort's poisonings run past what the Count intended, threatening the innocent Valentine and killing the child Édouard, so the machine gets away from its maker. That single move converts a cold competence-fantasy into real suspense and real moral weight simultaneously.
bond Dumas bonds us to a protagonist who spends the entire middle MASKED and COLD — the exact problem BURGUNDY has — and he does it with a three-valve system. VALVE 1, FRONT-LOADED INNOCENCE: before any machinery, the Marseille opening gives us pure young Edmond — generous to his dying captain, tender with his old father, guileless, in love, one day from captaincy and marriage — and then strips it all at his own betrothal feast. We love him BEFORE he is wronged, so the wrong (and later the mask) lands on an established attachment. The bond is banked early and spent across the long cold middle. VALVE 2, TEACHING-AS-LOVE IN THE CRUCIBLE: the Abbé Faria relationship in the Château d'If is the most intimate bond in the book, and it is built entirely out of PEDAGOGY — Faria is the first person to SEE Edmond's mind and cultivate it; education is how love is transmitted, because neither man can offer anything else. When Faria dies it is devastating precisely because being-taught was being-seen. VALVE 3, THE SECRET-GENEROSITY CHANNEL ONLY THE READER WITNESSES: throughout the cold Parisian middle the Count anonymously rescues the Morrel family (the one man who was kind to his father), an act of pure love done in secret that no character attributes to him — the reader alone holds it, and it is the reservoir that keeps the avenger human while his face stays a mask. Two supporting moves keep the bond alive across the freeze: MASK-CRACKS — Dumas engineers specific scenes (with Mercédès, with Maximilian Morrel, with Haydée) where Dantès-the-man breaks through Monte-Cristo-the-instrument, and we bond in the fissures, not the surface; and HOLDING THE WARM PAST AGAINST THE COLD PRESENT via memory, so the reader keeps grieving the boy the instrument used to be. The deepest bonding beat is the near-recognition: Mercédès sees through the mask. Being SEEN by the one person who loved him nearly dissolves the whole machine — the yearning to be seen and the danger of being seen are the same beat.

Run at minimum three interleaved fuses on different lengths: (a) the FATHER-CLOSING-IN thread (notices Mira's overspending → sends the finance minister to audit the ledgers → gripes to Burgundy → Burgundy deflects/offers to run the audit himself), (b) the MIRA-SMUGGLING-EXPOSURE thread (her shipments getting too large, the cargo-field blind spot, a suspected informer), and (c) the DEPOT-EMPTYING thread (the militant wanting weapons, the Ghai woman who knows the way in, the drilling). The father grousing to his son about the steel-and-bearings ledger — to the son who ORDERED the steel — is a perfect Dumas irony beat: the powerful man investigating his way toward the truth that will destroy him, one council-gripe at a time, while the reader alone holds both halves. engine The middle (the entire Aurdwynn province section, ~60% of the book) runs on ONE ratchet: Baru can only get close enough to destroy the Empire by serving it more brilliantly — so her competence and her exposure rise on the exact same curve, and every "win" is also a tightening. As Imperial Accountant she must crush corruption, master the province's finances, and prove herself the Masquerade's best technocrat; each success buys her the standing that lets her go deeper undercover into the rebellion she plans to both lead and betray. Crucially the engine is a GAP that widens: the mask (beloved rebel leader, lover of Duchess Tain Hu, true believer) and the plan (betray them all to be admitted to the Empire's inner circle of cryptarchs) drift further apart the more successful she is, because the betrayal is only valuable to the Empire if the rebellion she sells out is real and total. So the deeper she commits — the more genuine her love and loyalty become — the more monstrous the planned treason, and the reader's dread is the sound of two things she cannot keep together accelerating toward collision. Dickinson also keeps a rotating cast of ambiguous watchers (Cairdine Farrier, Xate Yawa, Cattlson) who may be handlers, plants, or rivals; Baru never knows who is testing her, so paranoia is a constant background pressure that spikes whenever she makes a move.
bond Four moves, stacked. (1) WOUND BEFORE MASK: the opening section shows the Empire arriving on her home island Taranoke, rewriting her people's language, sexuality and economy, and disappearing one of her fathers — all before she can consent or resist. (2) THE SECRET ONLY THE READER HOLDS: the narration is close third, and we are the SOLE keepers of her true purpose — every public scene has a private meaning only we track. That labor of secret-keeping, chapter after chapter, is the bond — dramatic irony deployed as intimacy and complicity, not as suspense. (3) YEARNING LOCATED AT THE POINT OF MAXIMUM DANGER: Baru is a lesbian in an empire that surgically "corrects" it, so her desire — for Tain Hu especially — is both where her interiority is rawest and where discovery would kill her. Feeling and exposure share one location; the reader feels her most exactly where she is least safe, which fuses tenderness to dread. We bond to her through the one outlet the mask permits.

The Dispossessed (Ursula K. Le Guin), read as the primary structural model, with The Lathe of Heaven as the secondary engine model. The Dispossessed supplies the braided architecture, the object-of-the-machine dawning, the un-seeing-parent wound, and the closing refusal-of-tribute gesture; Lathe supplies the recurring-session ratchet that maps almost one-to-one onto the training rotations.
engine Le Guin does not escalate with chases; she escalates with EPISTEMIC PRESSURE. The engine of The Dispossessed's middle is the slow conversion of "I am an honored guest" into "I am a possession." Each Urras chapter peels one layer: the luxury, then the surveillance, then the hidden poverty behind the wealth, then the discovery that his physicist hosts are competing to own his General Temporal Theory, then the state's true military/proprietary intent. The tension is the closing gap between what Shevek believes about his situation and what is actually true, and the reader is kept a half-step ahead of him, so we feel dread as he walks toward each recognition. Lathe runs the same pressure as a mechanical RATCHET: recurring therapy sessions, each one "solving" a problem and manufacturing a worse one (overpopulation cured by a plague that already killed billions, racism cured by making everyone grey, war cured by an alien invasion), with Haber's power visibly increasing every cycle, counting down to the moment he will no longer need Orr at all. TRANSPOSED: BURGUNDY's middle should ratchet on TWO counters rising in counterpoint on the rails of the training/building alternation. Counter A (knowledge) climbs through the rotations, each three-week posting peeling a layer of the world AND a layer of Burgundy's own captivity, exactly as each Urras chapter peels Urras and Shevek at once. Archives (the bearded archivist) gives him the Great Rupture and the proxy war, and the archivist's WORRY when the boy asks political questions is the first layer of the cage becoming visible. External Affairs (Schwartz) gives him the tribute economy, the vassal worlds, the ocean-protein planet, and with it the recognition that Dross is a mine another man's hand is on. The defense/King lessons give him the locked-away army, the depot, the red phone, and the father's doctrine ("I know what is moving under this planet before it moves"). Counter B (exposure) climbs through the build nights: material ordered, robots multiplied, the depot slowly emptied. The escalation is that KNOWING and DOING rise together and both converge on the Vicentennial, so the reader feels the walls approaching from two directions at once. The single most important Le Guin move to steal: the escalation is not "will he get caught," it is "how completely will he understand what he is before the door closes" — and the getting-caught pressure rides on top of that deeper current, never replacing it.
bond Le Guin bonds us to Shevek through six specific, repeatable moves. (1) Deep free-indirect interiority: his mind is the home base, we live inside the idealism and the reasoning, so his thought is more intimate than his action. (2) Origin-formation chapters: the Anarres childhood strand lets us watch the boy become the man, and witnessed early vulnerability creates a debt of intimacy the present-day chapters spend. (3) Somatic grounding: the world is felt through his skin, his nausea on Urras, the cold, the hunger during the famine years, so the reader's body is enlisted. (6) Moral loneliness: he is the one who sees clearly, and the isolation of conscience is its own bond. Lathe adds one more: George Orr's gentleness and horror at what is done THROUGH him, the decency of a man used as an instrument, and his solitary burden of remembering the world's prior versions that no one else can share. TRANSPOSED: Burgundy's home-base interiority is the ENGINEER'S EYE — he reads the world as mechanism (the manifest losing weight at every transit joint, the hull built to carry and never fight, the depot's unguarded back), and that habit of seeing is both his character and, per canon, how the geopolitics reaches the reader. Origin-intimacy is the age-12 mineshaft memory, the honey lozenge crushed under the father's heel — witnessed vulnerability that seeds everything.
