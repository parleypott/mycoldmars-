Welcome to PaperEdit ✎

A visual canvas for brainstorming and structuring your story — cards, frames, and acts, all connected.

Cards are your ideasDouble-click anywhere on the canvas to drop a new card and start typing.
Frames & Acts organize themGroup related cards into a Frame, or chain Frames together into an Act to build your sequence.
Connections show relationshipsUse the connect tool to link cards, then export the whole thing as a script or NLE timeline markers.
