Which countries are electoral democracies?

V-Dem sorts every country into one of four regimes by how free and fair its elections are, and whether the rule of law and rights that make a democracy liberal hold underneath them. An electoral democracy clears the bar for clean, multiparty elections; a liberal democracy also protects minorities and constrains executive power. Hover any country. Click a category to isolate it.
